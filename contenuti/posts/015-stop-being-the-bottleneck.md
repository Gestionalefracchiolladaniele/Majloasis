# Post 015 — "Prompting is already over" (prompt → /goal) [PUBBLICATO lug 2026]

> Hot take on-brand: ribalta l'idea che "prompt bene = sei avanti". La verità contrarian è che il
> prompting è il PRIMO step, non il traguardo, e chi lo tratta come traguardo diventa il collo di
> bottiglia del proprio sistema. Angolo genuinamente dell'utente: lui i loop li SCRIVE già (codifica
> dal telefono, sistemi che girano senza lui) → autorità vissuta, non teoria copiata dal video.
> Safe: ragionamento su come si costruisce con l'AI, zero progetti/scraping/dashboard.

## Testo (pronto da pubblicare) — versione aura [FOCUS: prompt è morto → adesso c'è il loop]

```
Prompting is already over.

Not because it stopped working. Because it was never the finish line. It was the first step.

Here is what the old way looked like. You spot the work. You write the prompt. You wait. You read what comes back. You decide if it's good. You paste it somewhere. You start the next one. Every round needs you in the chair. The better the model got, the more that hurt, because there was only one of you.

Here is what replaces it. You don't write the prompt anymore. You write the goal. The rules, and a way for the system to check its own work. Then you walk away. It prompts itself, over and over, and the work still gets done. You are just not there for it.

One simple command, /goal, changes everything.

Same model. Same person.

That is the whole jump. Before, you paid the thinking cost every single day. Now you pay it once, up front, and stop being the thing in the middle wiring the AI to the actual work.

How long until none of us are the ones writing what to do anymore?
```
