## Testo (pronto da pubblicare)

```
Everyone says AI automates tasks. That's the small story.

The real shift: AI is starting to automate decisions. Judgment, not just labor.

Software already ate the actions. Now models are eating the decisions underneath them (see below).

So the real question isn't "can AI do this task?" It's "which decisions do you trust it with, and which ones cost too much to get wrong?"

That line, what to delegate and what to own, is different for everyone.

Where do you draw it?
```
