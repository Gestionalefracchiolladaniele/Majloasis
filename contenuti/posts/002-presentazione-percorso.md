## Testo pubblicato

```
I started by automating boring tasks in a company. Nothing fancy, just the most basic stuff.

But I kept going.

Every project, I got a little better. I started building with Lovable. Then I moved to Claude Code, and that's when it clicked. Suddenly I could take an idea and ship a complete product, on my own: frontend, backend, mobile, automation, launch.

AI didn't just save me time. It unlocked what I could actually build.

So I went independent. One person, doing what used to take a team.

Now I want a bigger arena.

I'm heading to Dubai. Not for the skyline, but because it's a place that rewards people who can actually do the work. Competence over connections. Execution over talk. That's where I want to build next.

I know what I can build. Now I want to prove it somewhere new.

If you build things, or you're making things happen in the UAE, let's stay connected. The new journey starts here.
```
